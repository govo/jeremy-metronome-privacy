常见问题

点“开始录音”没有反应

  • 在系统设置中确认已允许 Acapella 使用麦克风；首次录音时应用会主动请求授权；
  • iOS：设置 > Acapella > 麦克风；
  • macOS：系统设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 麦克风。

想录其它 App 或系统的声音

  • macOS：需先允许“屏幕与系统音频录制”权限（系统设置 > 隐私与安全性），在录音器中选择“系统音频”或指定某个 App；
  • iOS：在录音器中选择“系统音频”并开始录制，iOS 会引导你开启一次屏幕广播。

分轨效果不理想或处理很慢

  • 所有处理都在本机完成，时长与歌曲长度和设备性能有关，请保持应用在前台并接通电源；
  • 人声突出的混音分轨效果更好；人声很轻或重度失真的音源效果会打折扣；
  • 细分声部（吉他、弦乐等）需要先下载对应模型（Pro）。

模型下载失败或很慢

  • 在应用“设置 > 下载源”中切换镜像源后重试；
  • 确认设备剩余存储空间足够；
  • 已下载的模型可以删除后重新下载。

购买 Pro 后功能没有解锁

  • 在应用“设置”中点击“恢复购买”；
  • 确认当前 Apple ID 与购买时使用的账户一致；
  • 重新启动应用后再试一次；如仍未解锁，通过上方邮箱联系我们，邮件会自动带上订阅诊断信息。

如何取消订阅

  • iOS：系统设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅；
  • macOS：App Store > 账户 > 管理订阅。

联系时建议提供

  • 通过应用内“设置 > 联系我们”发邮件，会自动附带版本与订阅诊断；
  • 问题的具体描述与复现步骤；
  • 如方便，请附上截图或录屏。

Frequently Asked Questions

Tapping “Record” does nothing

  • Make sure Acapella is allowed to use the microphone in system settings; the App requests access on the first recording;
  • iOS: Settings > Acapella > Microphone;
  • macOS: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

I want to record other apps or system audio

  • macOS: allow the “Screen & System Audio Recording” permission first (System Settings > Privacy & Security), then choose “System Audio” or a specific app in the recorder;
  • iOS: pick “System Audio” in the recorder and start — iOS will walk you through a one-time screen broadcast.

Separation results are poor or slow

  • All processing runs on your device; time depends on song length and device performance. Keep the App in the foreground and connected to power;
  • Vocal-forward mixes separate better; sources with quiet or heavily distorted vocals degrade results;
  • Finer splits (guitars, strings, etc.) require downloading the corresponding model first (Pro).

Model downloads fail or are slow

  • Switch to a mirror in Settings > Download Source and try again;
  • Make sure your device has enough free storage;
  • Downloaded models can be deleted and re-downloaded at any time.

Pro features are not unlocked after purchase

  • Tap “Restore Purchases” in the App’s Settings;
  • Make sure the current Apple ID matches the one used for the purchase;
  • Restart the App and try again. If it is still locked, email us — the message will include subscription diagnostics automatically.

How do I cancel a subscription?

  • iOS: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions;
  • macOS: App Store > Account > Manage Subscriptions.
