常见问题

检测不到声音，或读数不断跳动

  • 在系统设置中确认已允许 PTuner 使用麦克风；
  • macOS 上在应用“设置 > 音频输入”中选择正确的输入设备；
  • 靠近乐器，减少环境噪声与回声；
  • 拨弦或吹奏后保持音持续一小段时间，避免短促的敲击声。

调音结果和预期不一致

  • 确认“参考音高”符合你的演奏习惯，默认是 A4 = 440 Hz；
  • 确认所选乐器与调弦方式正确，或改用自由调音；
  • 观察音分偏差，轻微偏差属于正常范围。

购买 Pro 后功能没有解锁

  • 在应用“设置”中点击“恢复购买”；
  • 确认当前 Apple ID 与购买时使用的账户一致；
  • 重新启动应用后再试一次。

如何取消订阅

  • iOS：系统设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅；
  • macOS：App Store > 账户 > 管理订阅；
  • 永久版为一次性买断，不存在自动续期。

Frequently Asked Questions

No sound is detected, or the reading keeps jumping

  • Make sure PTuner is allowed to use the microphone in system settings;
  • On macOS, select the correct input device in Settings > Audio Input;
  • Move closer to the instrument and reduce ambient noise and echo;
  • Let the note sustain for a moment rather than using a short percussive attack.

The tuning result does not match what I expect

  • Check that the reference pitch matches your playing, A4 = 440 Hz by default;
  • Confirm the selected instrument and tuning, or use free tuning;
  • Check the cent deviation; a small deviation is normal.

Pro features are not unlocked after purchase

  • Tap “Restore Purchases” in the App’s Settings;
  • Make sure the current Apple ID matches the one used for the purchase;
  • Restart the App and try again.

How do I cancel a subscription?

  • iOS: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions;
  • macOS: App Store > Account > Manage Subscriptions;
  • The Lifetime option is a one-time purchase and does not renew.
